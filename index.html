Welcome to the O.B. Montessori Center in the Philippines!

We are an active pre-k to 12 co-educational school, offering a possible alternative to international schools. At OBMC, we blend tradition and creativity to prepare a child for life in the future. Our mission is to transform our students into self-developing and productive individuals who can succeed and influence the future of our world. Explore our programs and discover the community that makes OBMC a unique place for a child to learn, grow and develop.
